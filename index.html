Privacy Policy — InkReader

Last Updated: October 6, 2026 • Offline-First • Zero Telemetry

InkReader is an offline-first Android e-reader application built by Fiksr. We believe your reading habits, library, annotations, and AI queries are strictly personal. InkReader does not collect analytics, track device identifiers, or operate cloud servers that log your activity.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or advertising IDs.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs embedded in the app.
  • No Reading Telemetry: What books you read, how long you read, your reading speed, and bookmarks remain entirely on your local device.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • E-books & Documents: Imported EPUB files are kept in private local app storage.
  • Reading Progress & Stats: Bookmarks, reading heatmaps, words-per-minute calculations, and annotations are saved in your local database.
  • Custom Files: User-imported fonts (.ttf/.otf) and ambient audio tracks remain on your local storage.

3. Network Connections & AI Providers

Network activity only occurs when you intentionally trigger features that interact with external services:

  • AI Reading Companion: When you use AI features (e.g. "Catch Me Up" summaries or character explanations), requests are sent directly from your device over HTTPS to your chosen provider (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or DeepSeek/OpenRouter). API keys are stored privately on your device.
  • Offline Neural TTS: Voice models (Supertonic, Kokoro, Piper) are downloaded on-demand from public repositories. Once downloaded, all speech synthesis runs 100% offline on your device CPU. No audio or text is sent to cloud servers during reading.
  • OPDS Catalogs & Cloud Sync: Connections to OPDS catalogs (e.g. Standard Ebooks, Calibre) or sync services (BookOrbit, KoSync) occur directly between your device and that specific server address.

4. Android Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: User-triggered AI queries, OPDS browsing, sync, and one-time TTS voice downloads.

5. Contact

For questions or feedback, visit github.com/fiksr/inkreader or email contact@fikser.xyz.
